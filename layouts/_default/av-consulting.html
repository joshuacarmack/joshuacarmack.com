About

Experienced. Practical. Technical.

I've been running sound, designing systems, and solving live production problems for 17 years. I started volunteering on my church's tech team as a kid and never stopped — today I serve as Technical Director, overseeing audio, video, lighting, and broadcast for weekly services and events.

My 8 years in professional IT service adds a layer most AV consultants don't have: a deep understanding of network infrastructure, signal reliability, and system integration that spans from the patch bay to the cloud.

Whether you're planning a new install, struggling with a system that never sounds right, or need someone who can speak fluently with both your AV vendor and your IT team — I can help.

What I Do

Services

🔊

Audio Consulting

System design, speaker placement, signal flow, and equipment selection for new installs and upgrades — sized and scoped for your space and budget.

🎬

Video Consulting

Camera systems, switching, display setups, and signal routing for livestream, IMAG, and recording workflows. Built to be reliable and easy for your team to operate.

💡

Lighting Consulting

Fixture selection, placement, and control system recommendations for stage, architectural, and broadcast environments — designed to serve both live audiences and camera.

🎚️

PA System Tuning

Room analysis and system optimization using measurement tools — EQ, delay alignment, crossover tuning, and gain structure to get the most out of your existing rig.

🔧

Troubleshooting & Repair

System not behaving? I diagnose audio, video, and signal path issues and get you to a resolution — whether it's a config fix, a failed component, or a wiring problem buried in the wall.

🤝

Ongoing Support & Training

Team training, system documentation, evaluations, and ongoing technical support. Great for volunteer-run teams who want to level up without starting over.
