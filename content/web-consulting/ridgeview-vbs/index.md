## The Problem

Vacation Bible School registration had always been handled with paper forms at the door. Parents filled out forms the first day, staff entered the data into spreadsheets, and coordinators spent time manually sorting kids into classes by age group and tracking allergies, emergency contacts, and permission notes by hand.

The process worked — but it was slow, error-prone, and left coordinators scrambling at the start of each day.

## What I Built

A simple, mobile-friendly online registration system that lets parents register their children before VBS begins and gives coordinators a clean dashboard to manage everything.

### Parent Registration

Parents fill out a form online in advance. The form collects child and family information — name, age, grade, parent contacts, emergency contacts, medical notes, and permission acknowledgments. Multiple children from the same family can be registered in a single session. Confirmation is sent automatically after submission.

### Coordinator Dashboard

Coordinators log in to see all registrations in one place. Children are automatically sorted into class groups based on age and grade. Coordinators can view rosters by class, search for individual registrations, add notes, and mark check-in status each day. Allergy and medical notes are prominently displayed on each child's record.

### Reports and Exports

Daily attendance can be exported for records. Class rosters can be printed directly from the dashboard for teachers who prefer paper in the room.

## Tech Stack

Built with PHP and MySQL, hosted on shared hosting. Designed to be low-maintenance — coordinators need no technical background to use the admin interface.

## The Result

Registration moved out of the first-morning chaos and into the days before VBS. Coordinators arrived on day one with rosters already sorted and printed, parents felt better knowing their information was collected carefully, and the staff had more time to focus on the actual program.
