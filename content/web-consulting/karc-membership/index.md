## The Problem

Like a lot of volunteer-run organizations, the club was managing membership in a combination of spreadsheets, email threads, and personal notes held by whoever happened to be serving as secretary that year. When officers changed, institutional knowledge walked out the door. Dues tracking was inconsistent, the member roster was rarely fully accurate, and there was no good way to look up a member's contact information or license details quickly.

## What I Built

A straightforward web-based membership management system sized right for a small club — not enterprise software, just a clean tool that replaces the spreadsheets and makes it easy to keep member records accurate.

### Member Directory

All club members are in one searchable directory. Each record includes contact information, callsign, license class, and membership status. Officers can view and update records from any device.

### Dues Tracking

The system tracks membership dues by year. Officers can see at a glance who has paid, who is lapsed, and who is new. Renewal reminders can be sent from the dashboard.

### Self-Service Profile

Members can log in to update their own contact information and verify their record — reducing the burden on officers to chase down outdated info.

### Admin Tools

Officers have access to a simple admin panel for managing the roster, recording payments, sending announcements to the membership list, and exporting data for club records.

## Tech Stack

PHP backend with MySQL, designed for straightforward hosting and minimal ongoing maintenance. Built with the expectation that the people managing it are hobbyists, not IT staff.

## The Result

The club now has a single source of truth for membership. Officer transitions are smoother because the data lives in the system rather than in someone's personal files. Dues collection improved because it was easier to see who needed to renew.
