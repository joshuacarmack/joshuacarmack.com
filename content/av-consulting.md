## About

With 17 years of hands-on experience in professional audio and video production and 8 years in professional IT service, I bring a rare combination of deep technical knowledge and real-world troubleshooting expertise to every project.

I've spent years behind mixing consoles, designing signal chains, and tuning live sound systems in everything from small sanctuaries to large multi-venue facilities. Whether you're building something new from the ground up or trying to get an existing system to finally perform the way it should, I can help.

## Services

### Audio Consulting

System design and recommendations for new installs or upgrades — speaker placement, signal flow, equipment selection, and integration planning tailored to your room and budget.

### Video Consulting

Camera systems, display setups, switching, and signal routing. Whether you're running livestream, IMAG, or a dedicated recording workflow, I'll help you build a reliable, scalable video system.

### Lighting Consulting

Lighting design guidance for stage, architectural, and broadcast environments. From fixture selection to control system recommendations, I'll help you get the look you're after with the gear that makes sense for your space.

### PA System Tuning

Room analysis and system optimization using measurement tools to get the most out of your existing rig — EQ, delay alignment, crossover tuning, and gain structure.

### Troubleshooting & Repair Coordination

System not behaving? I diagnose audio, video, and signal path issues and help you get to a solution — whether that's a configuration fix, a component replacement, or a referral to the right vendor.

### General AV Support

Ongoing support, training for your volunteer or staff team, documentation, and system evaluations. If it involves audio, video, or lighting, reach out and let's talk.
